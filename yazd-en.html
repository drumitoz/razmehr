Historic City of Yazd

The story

The 1956 aerial photograph shows the compact city and its network of lanes. The later view brings us down to the earthen walls and shaded passages. UNESCO recognizes Yazd for its earthen architecture, windcatchers and qanat water system, shaped by a dry climate.

Amir Chakhmaq Complex

The story

Amir Chakhmaq is part of Yazd's shared urban memory and ceremonial life. Its tiered façade appears in both the 1970 archive photograph and a 2016 view. The square and surrounding structures remain among the city's best-known landmarks.

03 / 03

Ziaieh School

The story

In Fahadan, Ziaieh School is also popularly called ‘Alexander's Prison’. That name should not be taken as proof that it served as a prison in Alexander's time. The photographs show the building in the 1970s and in 2016.

RAZMEHR · YAZD

More places to discover

Fahadan Quarter

Walk among Fahadan's shaded passages, earthen walls and traditional houses.

Jameh Mosque of Yazd

The mosque's soaring entrance and tilework make it a landmark of old Yazd.

Dowlat Abad Garden

Water, trees and a tall windcatcher shape the experience of this Persian garden.

Yazd Fire Temple

The fire temple offers a glimpse into Yazd's living Zoroastrian heritage.

Towers of Silence

On the hills outside the city, the towers recall former Zoroastrian funerary practices.

Yazd Water Museum

In a historic house, the museum traces how qanats sustained life in an arid city.

Mirror and Lighting Museum

Mirrored decoration and lighting objects offer a different view of Yazd's decorative arts.

Lari House

Its courtyard, windcatchers and surrounding rooms evoke life in a traditional Yazd home.

Davazdah Imam Shrine

This domed brick shrine is a memorable stop on a walk through the old city.

Khan Bazaar

The bazaar's passages preserve the atmosphere of trading in historic Yazd.

Six Windcatchers Cistern

Six windcatchers rising above this cistern form a distinctive Yazd silhouette.

Vaght-o-Sa'at Square

The clock structure and nearby lanes make this square an easy stop during a city walk.

Malek-ol-Tojjar House

This historic merchant's house retains the feel of an urban courtyard residence.

Old City Walls

Surviving stretches of wall evoke the boundaries of the earlier city.

Zarch Qanat

The Zarch qanat is part of Yazd's long story of adapting to scarce water.

Narin Castle, Meybod

This adobe citadel rises above Meybod and defines part of its skyline.

Meybod Adobe Icehouse

Meybod's adobe icehouse recalls ways of storing ice in a hot desert climate.

Meybod Dovecote

The dovecote's many nesting spaces reveal a practical side of local architecture.

Chak Chak Shrine

Set against the mountains, Chak Chak is a Zoroastrian pilgrimage site.

Historic Kharanaq

Kharanaq's earthen lanes and old village fabric offer another face of the region.

Abarkuh Cypress

The ancient cypress is a beloved natural landmark of Abarkuh.

Aghazadeh House, Abarkuh

Its windcatcher and courtyard make Aghazadeh House a memorable Abarkuh landmark.

Pahlavanpur Garden

In Mehriz, trees and flowing water invite a pause at Pahlavanpur Garden.

Saryazd Castle

Saryazd's adobe walls and inner passages make the fortress worth a detour.

Jameh Mosque of Fahraj

Fahraj's brick mosque offers a quiet stop while exploring villages near Yazd.

Make room for Yazd

Coming to Razmehr to learn? Leave some time to discover Yazd as well. Every lane offers another reason to linger.
