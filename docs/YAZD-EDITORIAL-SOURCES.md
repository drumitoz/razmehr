# Yazd Through Time: source ledger

The four static language pages are generated by `python scripts/build-yazd.py`. Do not hand-edit the generated HTML. The section contains three sourced photo stories and a 28-place directory. The other 25 places have brief visitor notes, not photo-history stories; do not imply that archived photographs have been verified for them.

The supplied images were converted to WebP and resized for this site. Dates identify the photographs, not the construction dates of the buildings. The views are not aligned before/after comparisons.
